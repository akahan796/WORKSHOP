# Workshop AI — working spec

A OneStream app for organizing and running UX design-thinking workshops live, with
every attendee on their own screen. Based on the *Design Thinking Workshop Runbook*.

## Decisions (2026-10-28)

| Topic | Decision |
|-------|----------|
| Scope | Real working app, built in phases. |
| Backend | **Supabase**: Postgres, Realtime, Storage, Auth. Schema in `supabase/schema.sql`. |
| Sketches | Participants generate Crazy 8s / solution sketches in **their own AI tool** (skill files handed out ahead of time) and **upload** the images into the app. |
| Research share out | Researcher **uploads a PDF** (PPT → PDF); the app shows it as slides that **follow the presenter**, with the sticky capture panel beside them. |
| Front end | Build-less React 18 + `htm` from esm.sh (same as LCM). No Node needed. Arial font, brand `#0A5DFF`. |
| Hosting | Vercel, deployed by the GitHub Action on every push to `main`. |

## Roles

- **Organizer**: a staff account (email + password). Creates workshops, writes the intro, sets the agenda, invites people, runs the room (advances steps, starts timers, reveals boards).
- **Researcher**: invited to one workshop by link. Uploads and presents the research deck.
- **Participant**: invited to one workshop by link; discipline is **Product Design / Product Management / Engineering**. Sees only that workshop.
- **Decider**: a flag on any member (human or synthetic). Their votes get a distinct purple and extra weight, and they make the final call.
- **Synthetic participant** (phase 2): an AI member generated by the organizer to fill seats. It writes stickies, HMWs and votes through the Claude API.

## Guest access

The organizer adds a person, and the app creates a member row with a random `invite_token`.
Their link is `/#/join/<token>`. Opening it signs the browser in anonymously (Supabase
anonymous auth), asks for name and discipline, then binds that browser to the member through
the `claim_invite` RPC. There is no password. Opening the link on another device rebinds it.
Email: **Send invite** opens a prefilled email for now (mailto). Phase 2 adds `api/invite.js`, which sends through Resend.

## Workshop flow (agenda steps)

| # | Key | Step | Phase |
|---|-----|------|-------|
| 1 | `intro` | Introductions and orientation (organizer intro, project, overview, rules, what to expect). Prefilled from setup. | 1 |
| 2 | `research` | UX Research Share Out. Synced PDF slides plus silent sticky capture (pain point / opportunity / observation), then Q&A. | 1 |
| 3 | `hmw` | How Might We. Each participant writes color-coded HMW stickies, with their research stickies alongside for reference. | 1 |
| 4 | `grid` | Impact/Effort Grid. All HMWs start in an unsorted pile; AI places each one in a quadrant, then the group adjusts. | 2 |
| 5 | `vote_hmw` | Dot vote on HMWs (Decider in purple), discuss, align on one HMW. Decider breaks ties. | 2 |
| 6 | `crazy8` | Crazy 8s. 8-cell sheet, 8-minute timer, upload one sketch per cell. | 3 |
| 7 | `share8` | Share Crazy 8s (3 minutes each, presenter spotlight). 3 votes per person on individual cells; own ideas allowed. | 3 |
| 8 | `solution` | Solution sketch (about 1 hour). Title, 3+ frames, pictures and words; riffing encouraged. | 3 |
| 9 | `review` | Review and finalize solution sketches. Comments on each other's. | 3 |
| 10 | `vote_solution` | Dot vote on solutions, share reasoning, Decider calls the direction. Up to 2 rounds. | 3 |
| 11 | `flow` | Align on details and user flow. AI drafts the flow from the chosen sketch and the group edits it. Non-design folks released afterwards. | 4 |
| 12 | `prototype` | AI generates an interactive prototype from everything above. | 4 |
| 13 | `design_review` | Design review with the group. | 4 |

The organizer can turn steps on or off and set minutes per step during setup.
Every screen follows `workshops.current_step` in real time.

## Phases

1. **Foundation (this build)**: auth, workshop setup and intro content, agenda, roster and invite links, guest join, live room with step sync and timers, steps 1 to 3. Includes a local **demo mode** (no Supabase needed; tabs sync through BroadcastChannel) for previewing and testing.
2. **Built 2026-10-28:** Impact/Effort grid with AI placement, HMW dot voting with the Decider (purple, 2× weight; human or AI final call), synthetic participants, and the Claude edge function (`supabase/functions/ai`). Still open from phase 2: emailed invites (Resend).
3. Crazy 8s, share-out, solution sketches, review, solution voting.
4. AI user flow, AI prototype generation, design review, workshop export.

## Setup checklist (one-time, user-side)

1. Create a Supabase project. In SQL Editor, run `supabase/schema.sql`.
2. In Authentication → Providers, enable **Email** (password) and **Anonymous sign-ins**.
3. Copy the project URL and **anon** key into `config.js` (they are public by design; RLS protects the data).
4. Vercel project `workshop` plus GitHub Action secrets (see README).
